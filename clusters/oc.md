# Method inventory — On Certainty

The base skill already draws its hinge-sensitivity from this book. Listed here are the moves that go
*beyond* the base skill's "notice what stands fast" — the ones that make hinges dynamic, and that
supply the groundlessness the core's refusals rest on. Section numbers are the standard §§.

## Moves not already (or only thinly) in the base skill

### 1. Hinges as the door's fixed points, not extra beliefs
- **Question it lets you ask:** Which propositions here are not *in* the game of doubt at all, but
  the fixed points the game *turns on*? (A hinge isn't a specially certain belief; it's what has to
  stay put for questioning to move.)
- **From the book:** the questions we raise and our doubts "depend on the fact that some propositions
  are exempt from doubt, are as it were like hinges" (§341); "If I want the door to turn, the hinges
  must stay put" (§343).
- **Serves:** Mode 1 (hinge classification); Mode 2 (finding the unquestioned fixed points a dispute
  rests on).

### 2. Riverbed: hard and fluid propositions trade places over time
- **Question:** Is this proposition currently acting as *bed* (a rule of testing) or as *water* (a
  claim being tested)? Could it shift — could a hardened rule go fluid, or a fluid claim harden?
- **From the book:** some propositions of the form of empirical propositions get "hardened and
  functioned as channels" for the fluid ones, and the relation alters with time — fluid hardens,
  hard becomes fluid (§96); the riverbed of thoughts may shift (§97); the same proposition may be
  treated now as something to test, now as a rule of testing (§98).
- **Serves:** Mode 2 (Engine C — how a decided edge case *hardens* into the new rule); Mode 1 (the
  same sentence, two functions).

### 3. Justification ends in acting, not in a last proposition
- **Question:** Where does the chain of reasons actually stop here — and does it stop at a further
  fact, or at something we *do*?
- **From the book:** giving grounds comes to an end, but the end "is not an ungrounded
  presupposition: it is an ungrounded way of acting" (§110); "at the foundation of well-founded
  belief lies belief that is not founded" (§253); "at the end of reasons comes persuasion" (§612).
- **Serves:** the core's "I will not hand you a foundation" refusal; Mode 2's Step-0 honesty about
  where a demand for justification bottoms out.

### 4. The game of doubting presupposes certainty; a doubt needs an end
- **Question:** Would this proposed doubt actually *do* anything — is there a practice of doubting
  here, or only the words of doubt? What would settle it, and does that already presuppose what's
  being doubted?
- **From the book:** "If you tried to doubt everything you would not get as far as doubting
  anything. The game of doubting itself presupposes certainty" (§115); "A doubt without an end is not
  even a doubt" (§625); the child learns by believing — "doubt comes *after* belief" (§160).
- **Serves:** Mode 1 (distinguishing real from performed doubt); Mode 2's guardrail that every
  reframe must be answerable, not an endless doubt.

### 5. World-picture as inherited background, learned by acting not by proof
- **Question:** Is this the *substratum* against which we distinguish true and false, rather than
  something itself established as true?
- **From the book:** "it is the inherited background against which I distinguish between true and
  false" (§94); the world-picture is the "matter-of-course foundation" for research that goes
  unmentioned, learned as a child, not invented (§167); it is not intrinsically obvious but "held
  fast by what lies around it" (§144).
- **Serves:** Mode 2 (surfacing the unstated background a whole field is standing on); Mode 1.

## How this cluster changes the base skill
The base skill treats hinges statically ("here the sentence functions as a hinge"). On Certainty
makes them *dynamic* (riverbed, hardening/fluid) and grounds the core's refusals in the fact that
justification ends in action, not in bedrock. Load this file for anything about doubt, certainty,
foundations, or the unquestioned background of a practice.
