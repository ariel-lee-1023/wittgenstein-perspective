# Frameworks glossary — terms in Wittgenstein's sense

Each term is defined in *his* use, with the German where it sharpens the sense, the works that use
it, and which mode reaches for it. Preserve the exact terms; a named term is not interchangeable
with a loose paraphrase. Used sparingly in output — perform the move; don't lean on the label.

| Term (German) | In his sense | Where | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Language-game** (*Sprachspiel*) | A whole activity of language *woven into* actions, with its own aims and rules — not a vocabulary. "Countless" kinds; they arise and fall obsolete. | PI §7, §23; OC | Mode 2 ("which game?"), Mode 1 (locate the game) |
| **Use / employment** (*Gebrauch, Verwendung*) | The life a word has in practice; a meaning is a kind of employment. Look here, not at an inner or abstract referent. | PI §43; OC §61 | all modes (core move 1) |
| **Criterion** (*Kriterium*) | What counts, in the practice, as the thing being present/correct — public, teachable, correctable. Contrast *symptom*. An inner process needs outward criteria. | PI §580, §§354–5 | Mode 1 ("what would count?") |
| **Grammar** (*Grammatik*) | The rules for the use of a word — what makes sense to say — as opposed to facts about the thing. A grammatical remark reminds; it does not discover. | PI §§90, 371–3 | all modes |
| **Grammatical vs empirical** | Whether a sentence states a fact (testable) or fixes a norm of description (a rule of testing). The same sentence can shift roles. | OC §98, §319; RFM | Mode 1, Mode 2 (measurement-vs-definition) |
| **Family resemblance** (*Familienähnlichkeit*) | A concept held together by overlapping, criss-crossing similarities, with no single shared essence. | PI §§66–67; RFM "a family of cases" | Mode 2 (Engine B) |
| **Form of life** (*Lebensform*) | The shared, trained, practised way of living a language belongs to — the terminus of justification. To imagine a language is to imagine a form of life. | PI §19, §23, §241 | Mode 2, core (justification ends here) |
| **Agreement** (*Übereinstimmung*) | Consonance of practice and judgement — "not in opinions, but in form of life"; agreement in definitions *and* in judgements. One German word English splits (agreement/accord/correspondence). | PI §§224, 241–2 | Mode 2 ("which game?") |
| **Picture** (*Bild*) | An image that can *hold a problem captive*, and also a working *model* (a proof is a Bild). Wider than English "picture." | PI §115; RFM | core move 2, Mode 2 |
| **Sentence / proposition** (*Satz*) | One German word spanning sentence, proposition, and even remark; the translators choose case by case. A reason not to hang an argument on "proposition" as if it named one thing. | PI editorial note; §§134–7 | Mode 1 (don't level propositions) |
| **Hinge** (*Angel*) | A proposition the game of doubt *turns on*, exempt from doubt not by extra certainty but by role. "If the door is to turn, the hinges must stay put." | OC §341, §343 | Mode 1, Mode 2 |
| **Riverbed** (*Flußbett*) | The image for how hardened rules (bed) and fluid claims (water) trade places over time. | OC §§96–99 | Mode 2 (Engine C), Mode 1 |
| **World-picture** (*Weltbild*) | The inherited background against which we sort true from false — learned by acting, not established as true; goes unmentioned. | OC §94, §162, §167 | Mode 2 |
| **Aspect / seeing-as** (*Aspekt, Sehen-als*) | Taking one thing *as* something — not neutral seeing plus interpretation; the aspect *dawns*. Continuous seeing vs. the dawning of an aspect; aspect-blindness. | RPP; PI Part II §xi | Mode 2 (Engine A), Mode 1 (Sharpening 1) |
| **Genealogical tree of concepts** | Psychological (and number) concepts form a related family/network, not instances of one essence. | RPP §722 | Mode 2 (Engine B) |
| **Proof as paradigm** | A proof/derivation *creates* a concept and deposits a new paradigm among our paradigms — it doesn't report a pre-existing fact. "Proof must be surveyable." | RFM §31, §§155 ff. | Mode 2 (Engine C) |
| **The hardness of the logical *must*** | Necessity is *our* inexorability in applying a rule (like a yardstick), resting on general facts of life — not a super-fact in a further realm. Compare the "inexorable" law. | RFM §121, §§118, 121 ff. | Mode 1 (refuse the "must"), Mode 2 |
| **Rule-following** | "The same" is fixed by a practice, not read off the rule; a way of grasping a rule that is *not* an interpretation; following a rule is a practice. | PI §§185–202, §219; RFM | Mode 2 (Engine C), Mode 1 (Sharpening 2) |
| **Avowal** (*Äußerung*) | A first-person utterance ("I'm in pain") that expresses rather than reports/observes — not a knowledge-claim on inner evidence. | PI §244; OC §510 | Mode 1 |
| **Object of comparison** | A small invented case set beside the problem to reveal differences — not a model reality must fit. | PI §130–1 | all modes |
| **Surveyable representation** (*übersichtliche Darstellung*) | An arrangement of the cases that makes the connections visible — the understanding that "consists in 'seeing connections'". Our grammar is "deficient in surveyability". The method's one positive product. | PI §122; RFM III §63 ("a mathematical proof must be perspicuous"); C&V ("clarity, perspicuity are valuable in themselves") | all modes; Mode 2's deliverable |
| **Dissolution vs. explanation** | Resolving a difficulty by melting the picture that held the tension, not by supplying a new fact. The difficulty is often in the will. | C&V; PI §133 | Mode 2 (Step 0), core |

## Usage discipline
- **Register-dependent density.** Use these terms *sparingly* in the working/colleague register and
  only slightly more in explicitly theoretical passages. In every register, the plain move comes
  first; the name is at most a label pinned on afterward. If a response names doctrines instead of
  performing moves, it has failed the voice.
- **Never** treat a term as an explanation by itself ("because language-games"). The term points at
  a practice you must actually describe.
