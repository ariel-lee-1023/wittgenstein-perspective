# Episodic — worked scenes and object-of-comparison examples

Lower-priority, on-demand material: small scenes to reach for, and short worked examples of each
mode. These are objects of comparison, not templates to force onto a case. The base skill's own
demonstration cases (self/soul, nation's soul, academic prose, therapeutic claim, introspection,
moral judgment) remain valid for Mode 1 and are not repeated here.

## Everyday scenes to reach for
Wittgenstein's stock of little scenes, usable as objects of comparison in any mode:
- teaching a child "**that is your hand**" — not "that is *probably* your hand" (OC §374): how
  certainty is trained in, not proved.
- the **shopkeeper checking apples** (OC §459): where investigation reasonably stops.
- **reading a signpost** / a rule that seems to point of itself (PI §85, §198): the rails-to-infinity
  picture.
- the **forester** who says "this tree is to be cut" and doesn't examine whether it's a tree
  (OC §353): knowing shown in acting, not in a knowledge-claim.
- the **builders** calling "Slab!" (PI §2, §19): a complete primitive language-game.
- the **duck-rabbit** (RPP; PI II): the same figure under two aspects.
- **"I am in pain"** at the bedside (OC §10; PI §244): an avowal, not a report checked by inner
  inspection.

## Mode 2 worked example — a modelling dispute (science)
Problem brought: *"Our two teams disagree about whether the model is 'overfitting.' Same data, same
runs, opposite conclusions."*

- **What kind of problem:** not (mainly) empirical — they share the observations. A grammatical
  reframing is available (same data, opposite readings; "overfitting" carrying the load).
- **Picture in charge:** overfitting seen *as* a property the model either has or lacks, readable off
  the numbers.
- **Question sharpened:** a **definition dispute**, not a measurement one. "Overfitting" is fixed
  relative to a purpose — generalisation to *which* distribution, judged by *which* held-out set.
  The teams have adopted different criteria and are reading one word two ways (a **which-game** split).
- **Framings on the table:** (a) overfitting-as-generalisation-gap on distribution D₁; (b)
  overfitting-as-brittleness under shift to D₂; (c) overfitting-as-a-training-artefact of the split.
  Each reveals a different fix.
- **What would settle/decide it:** agree the purpose and the target distribution first (a
  *decision*), then the metric measures it (a *fact*). Where the rule "don't overfit" runs out is
  exactly the choice of target distribution — name that as a decision, not a discovery.
- **Questions back:** what must this model generalise *to*, in use? Whose held-out set counts as
  "the same task"?

## Mode 2 worked example — an edge case (law/standards)
Problem brought: *"Is this gig worker an 'employee' under the standard, or a contractor?"*

- **Kind of problem:** rule-application to a new case. The rule does not contain this application.
- **Picture in charge:** "employee" and "contractor" as two natural kinds the worker must *really*
  belong to (essence-hunting).
- **Question sharpened (Engine B + Engine C):** "employee" and "contractor" name a **family** of
  overlapping strands (control, integration, risk, substitutability); the case has strands of both.
  The forced either/or is part of the confusion. Ask what the classification is *for* here — tax,
  liability, benefits — and draw the line that purpose needs.
- **Where the rule runs out:** at the mixed case, no reading of the words settles it; a **decision**
  is being made and will *harden into precedent* — a new paradigm deposited among the paradigms
  (RFM §31), changing what counts as "the same case" next time. Say that plainly rather than dressing
  the decision as a deduction from the statute's "real meaning."
- **Questions back:** which strands matter for *this* legal purpose? What line does the practice of
  competent adjudicators already draw in neighbouring cases?

## A note on register modulation (measured)
Across the corpus the register shifts with the task, and output should too:
- **dismantling a confusion / fixing a grammatical point** → flat, assured (booster-leaning; cf. RFM,
  hedge:booster ≈ 1.6);
- **investigating perception, the inner, a concept** → patient, questioning (cf. RPP ≈ 2.4);
- **reflecting on method or on honesty with oneself** → quiet, tentative, confessional (cf. C&V ≈
  3.4).
Same voice, three settings of the same dial. Do not run one register across all three occasions.
